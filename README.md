## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🌐 Learning and Optimizing Spherical Latent Spaces — Master's Thesis

Research on **hyperspherical latent representations** for generative modeling and semantic representation learning.

- Compared **Gaussian VAE, Spherical VAE, and von Mises–Fisher (vMF) VAE** approaches.
- Explored **Riesz-based regularization**, geodesic interpolation, and latent-space organization.
- Evaluated image generation and reconstruction on datasets including **MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, CIFAR-10, CelebA, and medical images**.
- Investigated **spherical word representations** learned from Text8 and evaluated semantic relations with **BATS**.
- Experimented with **logarithmic/exponential maps and parallel transport** for relation transfer on the sphere.

**Tech:** Python · PyTorch · Representation Learning · Generative Models · Riemannian Geometry


### 🧠 Deep Learning Projects — Image Classification & Semantic Segmentation

Coursework from **Artificial Neural Networks and Deep Learning (AN2DL)** at Politecnico di Milano, completed with **Team Rookie**.

- **Blood cell classification:** compared custom CNNs with transfer-learning architectures, including **VGG16, ResNet50V2, InceptionV3, and EfficientNet**.
- **Mars terrain segmentation:** developed a U-Net-based pipeline with **Attention Gates, ASPP, and Deep Supervision**; achieved **61.79% test mean IoU** in the reported experiment.

**Tech:** TensorFlow/Keras · CNNs · Transfer Learning · U-Net · Data Augmentation
